# Wedding Reception Playlist

Tom & Jessica — reception run-of-show playlist.

Built as a phased set, not a shuffle. Each phase has a job: get people talking, get them fed, get them on the floor, keep them there, send them home happy. Energy ramps once and never drops until last call. Approximate total: **4 h 50 min**, 76 tracks plus 4 spotlight songs, with a 10-track reserve.

Fill in the four spotlight songs before handing this to the DJ or building the Spotify/Apple queue. Everything else is ready to load.

---

## How to use this

- **Order matters within a phase.** Tracks are sequenced for tempo and key-adjacent transitions. Shuffle only inside a phase if you must, never across phases.
- **BPM column** is there so a DJ can beatmatch or so you can sanity-check the ramp. Rough numbers, not gospel.
- **Volume cue** tells whoever runs sound where the fader lives. Dinner is background. Peak is loud.
- **Reserve list** at the bottom is for requests and a floor that empties early. Slot them in, do not append.

## Do Not Play

Hand this list to the DJ with the playlist. Requests for these get a polite no.

- Chicken Dance, Macarena, YMCA, Cha Cha Slide. Line dances are covered by Cupid Shuffle and Wobble in reserve; the rest are a hard pass.
- Any song about a breakup framed as a love song. "Every Breath You Take", "I Will Always Love You", "Someone Like You".
- Anything by an artist either family has an issue with. Add names here: `________________`
- Explicit versions during Phases 1 through 4. Clean edits only until the peak set, then DJ discretion.

## Building the actual playlist

Fastest path: create one Spotify or Apple Music playlist per phase, named `Reception 1 - Cocktails`, `Reception 2 - Dinner`, and so on. Whoever runs sound switches playlists at each transition instead of scrolling one 80-track list. Keep the reserve as its own playlist.

If a DJ is running it, send this file and let them source the tracks. Ask for clean edits explicitly.
