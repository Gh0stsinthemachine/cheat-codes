# Relocation — Moving Abroad

Vetted tips for moving from the US to another country. Portugal-specific plans live in `/Claude/Portugal Planning/` (repo `portugal-d2`); this file holds the general, reusable parts.

### "Move abroad cheat code" (10-step viral list): half right, wrong order

A viral list: renew passport, open Schwab checking, get the FBI check, get a virtual mailbox in TX/FL/NV, port your number to Google Voice, apostille documents, pick a country, apply, fly, stop googling. The useful core is real. Three problems:
1. **The FBI check is not free.** It costs $18 (edo.cjis.gov), plus fingerprinting, plus a State Department apostille.
2. **Order is wrong.** Most visas want the FBI check under 90 days old at filing (Portugal: yes). Getting it at step 3, before choosing a country, means it expires. Request it about 2.5 months before filing.
3. **It skips the hard parts.** For a Portugal D2: NIF, a funded Portuguese bank account, business plan and investment declaration, travel insurance, the VFS appointment wait, pets, and US tax (FBAR, PFIC).

Keepers:
- **Schwab checking** for life abroad: refunds ATM fees worldwide.
- **Google Voice port, $20 one-time** (Google's help page). It keeps your US number for bank and IRS texts. Port it while still in the US. Some banks reject internet phone numbers for 2-factor codes, so test each bank before you leave.
- **A virtual mailbox** is an option for a US address. Some banks and brokers refuse mail-drop addresses, so a family member's address is safer where you have one.
